AI fundamentals for QA and tech learners

Build practical AI literacy before moving into prompts, agents, automation, and QA workflows.

Step 1: Core Concepts

  • AI is software that can classify, predict, generate, summarize, reason, or assist with decisions.
  • LLMs work with prompts, context, tokens, examples, and instructions.
  • AI output quality depends on clear context, good examples, and verification.
  • Use AI for brainstorming, summarizing, drafting, comparing, and explaining.

Step 2: How QA Uses AI

  • Summarize requirements and convert acceptance criteria into test ideas.
  • Create checklists, edge cases, test data, and exploratory charters.
  • Explain failures, logs, APIs, selectors, and automation code.
  • Prepare interview answers, learning plans, and daily work notes.

Step 3: Safety & Review

  • Do not paste passwords, tokens, private data, or production customer information.
  • Ask AI to list assumptions, missing context, risks, and verification steps.
  • Compare AI output with requirements before using it in real work.
  • Remember: AI drafts; humans validate, decide, and own the result.
